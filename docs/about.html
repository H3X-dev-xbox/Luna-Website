🌙 Our Story

Built by players.
For players.

Luna Game Store started as a simple frustration: why does finding and installing a game have to feel like fighting a bloated corporate launcher? So we built the alternative — clean, fast, and free.

Why Luna exists

Every major game launcher today is bloated. They run background services, show you ads you didn't ask for, force you to update when you just want to play, and treat game discovery as an algorithmic slot machine.

Luna takes the opposite approach. Minimal. Fast. Transparent. A dark, galaxy-themed interface that gets out of your way and lets you find the game you actually want — then install it without seven clicks through a maze of menus.

We don't repack games. We don't crack anything. We're a launcher and catalog — a cleaner front-end for the repacks that the community already trusts.

💎 What We Stand For

Our core values

Six principles that shape every decision we make — from design to deployment.

🎁

Free Forever

After beta, Luna is 100% free. No subscriptions, no paywalls, no "premium tier" hiding basic features behind a paywall.

⚡

Speed First

Luna launches in seconds, not minutes. No background telemetry, no bloat, no memory leaks eating your RAM while you game.

🔓

Transparent

No hidden data collection. No selling your information. No dark patterns. Just a launcher that does what it says.

🎮

Player-Focused

Every feature exists because it improves the player's experience — not because it improves someone's quarterly numbers.

🙌

Credit the Creators

FitGirl does the hard work. We just make it easier to find and install. All repack credit goes directly to her team.

🌌

Built for the Long Haul

Luna isn't a weekend project. It's a serious, sustainable tool for the PC gaming community — and it's here to stay.

🚀 The Journey

Where we've been

From a random idea to a real product — here's the road so far.

Phase 01
The Spark
Luna started as a personal frustration with existing launchers — a note in a phone, then a design mockup, then a real plan.
Phase 02
Foundation & Design
Locked in the galaxy theme, built the visual identity, and chose the tech stack: Python, CustomTkinter, and a FitGirl-powered catalog.
Phase 03
Core Development
Building the store UI, catalog fetcher, download engine, and installer. This is where we are right now.
Phase 04
Paid Beta
A limited, paid early access phase to fund server infrastructure and get real-world feedback before the public launch.
Phase 05
Free Public Launch
Luna becomes 100% free for everyone, forever. No subscriptions, no paywalls, no asterisks.
