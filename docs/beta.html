Beta Access · Not Yet Open

The Paid Beta
that funds the free future.

Luna will be 100% free forever. But building and running it isn't. To keep our servers alive during beta, we're opening a small number of paid early-access slots — a one-time cost that directly pays for infrastructure.

💡 Why is the beta paid?

Running a game launcher isn't free. Every user who downloads a game pulls massive amounts of bandwidth from our servers. If we opened the beta to everyone on day one, our infrastructure would collapse under the load — and nobody would get to play anything.

To keep Luna stable during beta, we're offering a small number of paid early-access slots. This isn't about profit — it's about survival.
