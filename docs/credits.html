⚖️ Legal

Disclaimers & policy

Transparency matters. Here's exactly what Luna is — and isn't.

🎯 What Luna Is

Luna Game Store is an independent launcher and catalog interface. It is a piece of software that helps users browse, discover, and install games that are hosted on third-party sites.

  • We provide a UI for browsing a public catalog
  • We provide a download manager for third-party files
  • We provide a launcher for installed games
  • We give full credit to the original repacker (FitGirl)

🚫 What Luna Is Not

Luna does not host, own, crack, or distribute any games. We have no control over the content of external sites or the files they serve.

  • We do not host any game files on our servers
  • We do not crack or bypass any DRM
  • We do not repack or modify game content
  • We do not sell, license, or monetize game content
  • We do not claim ownership of any third-party content

DMCA & Rights Holder Policy

If you are a rights holder and believe that any content linked from Luna infringes on your copyright, we will comply immediately. Luna takes intellectual property seriously and will remove any link or reference upon valid request.

Since Luna does not host any files directly, the actual removal of a game file must be directed to the original host (e.g. FitGirl, Archive.org). We will, however, remove any link from our catalog and website as soon as we are notified.

To submit a request, please reach out through our GitHub repository or the contact methods listed in our community section.

Respect the creators.

If you enjoy a game, support the developers. If you use FitGirl's repacks, consider supporting her too. Luna is just a doorway — the real work is done by the people behind the games.
